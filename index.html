About us

Carbase is a used car dealership and workshop in Tarpon Springs, Florida. We buy, inspect and sell trucks, passenger cars and electric vehicles from the brands drivers trust most.

Every car is checked before it goes on the lot, and we tell you plainly what we found. You get a clear price, the full vehicle history and no pressure.

Looking for something specific? Tell us the brand, budget and year, and we will source it for you.

We also work with the major U.S. auction platforms, including Manheim, Copart, IAAI and Adesa, as well as other auctions.

Auction services

We help you buy a car at auction: from finding the right one to a careful check before you bid.

01

Sourcing and buying from dealer auto auctions

Many good cars are sold only through closed, dealer-only auction platforms that regular buyers cannot access. Tell us the make, model, year and your budget, and we will find the car and buy it for you at the auction.

  • Search by your make, model, year and budget
  • A shortlist of the best matches with photos and condition reports
  • Clear advice and an honest opinion before you decide
  • We bid and buy the car at the auction for you
02

Vehicle inspection at the auction

Photos and condition reports do not always tell the whole story. Before you bid, we can check the car at the auction, where the auction allows it, and tell you what we really see.

  • Body, paint and signs of previous repairs
  • Interior, tires, lights and visible mechanical issues
  • Photo and video report with our honest opinion
